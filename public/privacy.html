Privacy

Short, because there isn't much.

What is stored

  • The two nicknames you type, and the eight numbers each person enters.
  • Two random links, one per person. Holding a link is the only way to see a session.
  • A salted hash of the address a session was created from, used only to limit how many sessions one connection can start per hour. It is erased after 24 hours.

What is not

  • No accounts, email addresses, cookies, analytics or trackers.
  • The only thing kept in your browser is your light or dark choice.

Where and for how long

  • Data lives on Cloudflare, in Western Europe.
  • A session is kept for 90 days, then handled as if deleted.

Deleting

  • Either person can tap "Delete names and links" on their session page at any time. Both links stop working immediately.
  • When a finished session is deleted or expires, its sixteen numbers and the day it was created are kept for statistics. Nothing that could identify either person is kept with them.
  • An unfinished session is simply removed.

Teams

  • A team stores its name, each member's nickname, each member's four numbers, and one link per member.
  • Everyone in the team sees the nicknames and who has answered. Only the person who set the team up sees the links. Numbers are shown only as the team's minimum, average and maximum, plus your own, which are kept on the device you answered from and never sent back out.
  • Before the reveal, you can leave if you haven't answered. After it, you can remove your name; your numbers stay in the averages, where they can't be told apart.
  • Expiry and deletion work as for sessions: 90 days, then the numbers are kept without names and everything else is removed.

Responsibility

  • Whoever creates a session or a team is responsible for what they type. Use nicknames, and tell the people you invite.
  • If a workplace uses team mode for its own purposes, that employer is the controller for that use, as with any tool.
