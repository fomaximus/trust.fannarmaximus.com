T = (C + R + I) / S

The Trust Equation

Three things that help, divided by one that hurts.

  • Credibility C

    Words. Can I believe what they say? Do they know their subject, and do they say "I don't know" when they don't?

  • Reliability R

    Actions. Can I depend on them? Do they do what they said, when they said?

  • Intimacy I

    Safety. Can I tell them something that matters without it coming back to bite me?

  • Self-orientation S

    Focus. How much of their attention is on themselves rather than on you? This one divides. High self-orientation undoes everything above it.

Each factor is scored 1 to 10. The result runs from 0.3 to 30.

The Trust Equation comes from The Trusted Advisor (Maister, Green & Galford, 2000). trustedadvisor.com/equation
